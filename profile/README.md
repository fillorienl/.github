## What we do

We help teams move from AI experiments to AI that does real work. That work is usually the slow, repetitive, rule-heavy tasks that cost people hours every week. We build solutions that keep running after we hand them over.

| Service | What it means |
|---|---|
| **AI Transformation** | Bring AI into existing workflows and engineering practices: drafting, sorting, extracting and checking. |
| **Data Migration** | Move, clean and modernize data and processes so they are ready for AI. |
| **AI Training** | Hands-on training so teams use AI safely and well in their own work. |
| **AI Consulting** | Vendor-neutral advice on strategy, feasibility and a roadmap. |
| **QA and Test Automation** | Automated test suites and QA processes. |
| **Cyber Security with AI** | AI-assisted threat detection, monitoring and response. |

Typical first projects: quotes and proposals, orders from email and PDF, invoice matching, customer emails, reporting and regression testing.

## How we work

1. **A 20-minute call** to find out whether AI is the right fix. If it isn't, we tell you.
2. **One clearly scoped process** with an agreed outcome.
3. **Build and integration** inside the tools you already use.
4. **Handover and training** so your team can run, maintain and extend it.

**What we believe**

- **Outcomes over hype.** We measure success in hours saved and errors avoided.
- **Reliable foundations first.** No lock-in, and no retainer required.
- **You own the data, the decisions and the risk.** We work in your environment where possible and follow the GDPR and the EU AI Act.
- **We're a small team of practitioners**, not a slide-deck agency.

## Open demos

We publish working demos of the patterns we build. In each one, AI reads, plain code decides, and a person approves. All companies and data in the demos are fictional.

| Repo | What it shows |
|---|---|

| [demo-email-triage](https://github.com/fillorienl/demo-email-triage) | Customer-service emails classified by AI, routed by code-based rules, and answered with drafts that a grounding check verifies before a person sends them. |
