## What we do

We help teams move from AI experiments to AI that does real work. That work is usually the slow, repetitive, rule-heavy tasks that cost people hours every week. We build solutions that keep running after we hand them over.

| Service | What it means |
|---|---|
| **AI Transformation** | Bring AI into existing workflows and engineering practices: drafting, sorting, extracting and checking. |
| **Data Migration** | Move, clean and modernize data and processes so they are ready for AI. |
| **AI Training** | Hands-on training so teams use AI safely and well in their own work. |
| **AI Consulting** | Vendor-neutral advice on strategy, feasibility and a roadmap. |
| **QA and Test Automation** | Automated test suites and QA processes. |
| **Cyber Security with AI** | AI-assisted threat detection, monitoring and response. |
